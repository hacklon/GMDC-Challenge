This submission presents an operational, physics-grounded machine learning system designed to forecast hourly particulate matter (PM2.5) concentrations across continuous monitoring stations in Mumbai. 2. Atmospheric Physics & ClimateVerse Integration (Novelty)

Mumbai's coastal meteorology creates distinct boundary-layer trapping events. Clean sea breezes alternate with dry continental land breezes, while cold nocturnal air drainage drops the planetary boundary layer height (PBLH) down to under 100 meters, trapping PM2.5 near ground level. We engineered four physical indices:

A. Ventilation Coefficient (Vc)
Vc = Wind Speed (m/s) × PBLH (m)

Measures the volume rate of air dispersal. Values < 2,000 m²/s indicate severe atmospheric trapping.

B. Thermal Inversion Index (Iinv)
Iinv = Psurface / (PBLH + 10)

Quantifies surface pressure capping that prevents vertical convective mixing during winter nights.

C. Orthogonal Wind Vectors (U & V)
U = -s·sin(θ), V = -s·cos(θ)

Distinguishes clean westerly maritime sea breezes from dirty north-easterly continental advection.

D. Atmospheric Stagnation Index
Sidx = 1000 / (Wind Speed × PBLH + 10)

Highlights calm, stagnant meteorological regimes where local emissions rapidly compound.

3. Machine Learning Quad-Ensemble Architecture (Functionality)

To guarantee minimum prediction variance and eliminate extreme outlier hallucinations, we trained four diverse model architectures on the 90-day post-monsoon window ending November 10, 2026:

  • Random Forest Regressor (35% weight): 150 regularized bagging trees capturing non-linear threshold triggers.
  • LightGBM Regressor (30% weight): Gradient-boosted decision trees with direct categorical station splits.
  • CatBoost Regressor (20% weight): Ordered target boosting resilient to target leakage on temporal series.
  • XGBoost Regressor (15% weight): Regularized extreme boosting optimizing squared-error loss.
Validation Performance: On the official competition 2-day reporting-delay backtest, our Quad-Ensemble achieved an RMSE of 8.755 across all four stations (Station 11606: 8.59, Station 11611: 8.75, Station 3409510: 8.58, Station 3409511: 9.09), outperforming the official starter baseline (9.06).
